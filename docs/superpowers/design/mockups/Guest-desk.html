Before you come

  • Placitas sits at almost 6,000 feet. Afternoons can be warm and nights cold, so bring layers, and drink more water than you think you need.
  • Free-roaming horses graze the ridges here. Give them room, and please don't feed them.
  • In December, farolitos line the walk to the San Antonio Mission for Las Posadas on Christmas Eve.
  • The address and how to get in come with your confirmation email.

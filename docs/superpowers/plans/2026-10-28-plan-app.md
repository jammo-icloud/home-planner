# Plan App Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace Trello: plans in the household log, a board of area rows × Planning | Doing with shelves, a Linear-shaped card with notes, checklist, attachments, people and money, ask-not-assign between members, and Claude research that lands as suggestions.

**Architecture:** Plans are new event ops folded into `state.plans` in `replay.ts`, in a new `plans` restore scope. Member edits are direct writes through `/api/plans/*` routes (they are not money; a preview step to tick a checklist box would be absurd), while Claude reaches the same operations through write tools that return proposals, so its research suggestions arrive as cards through the thread machinery that already exists. Attachments and contacts use the Synology clients from the Synology Sources plan. The UI is two new views in `site/index.html`: the board and the card.

**Tech Stack:** As the project: Node 26, `node:test`, single-file vanilla JS UI, no new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-28-home-planner-design.md` — Goals 2, 5; Anti-goals ("A thinking item gets nagged", "Assigning without asking", "A suggestion reads as a decision"); Decided › Plan; Decided › Contacts; Calls 3, 4, 5, 8, 10. Requires `2026-10-28-synology-sources.md` complete.

## Global Constraints

- Two levels, enforced in replay: a plan's `parent` must exist and must itself have no parent.
- A plan with children (a shelf) has no state of its own; `plan.state` on a shelf is refused in replay.
- States: `planning`, `doing`, `done`. The clock runs only in `doing` (`stateAt` is when it entered its current state).
- Chips are free text, never affect the column, never show age.
- Only the owner may `plan.ask`; only the asked may `plan.ask.accept` / `plan.ask.decline`. There is no other path onto another member's board.
- `plan.remove`, `plan.ask`, `plan.ask.decline`, `plan.detach`, `plan.unblock` require a reason (`REQUIRES_WHY`).
- Claude may not author any `plan.*` op directly; it proposes through `proposal.offer`.
- `PLANNER_ROOT` has no default; unset → Attachments off, the card says so.
- Attachments live at `${PLANNER_ROOT}/${household}/${plan}/${name}`; downloads are proxied through the app.
- Money on a card: a link to an envelope in a budget plus an optional estimate; shown figures come from `analytics.ts`.
- Areas come from `state.settings.areas`; a plan's area must be one of them.

## Review Focus

1. **A plan's parent is removed.** Children must not vanish or become orphans; `plan.remove` on a shelf with live children is refused. Pinned in task 1.
2. **The same plan is asked to two people, or re-asked while an ask is open.** One open ask per plan. Pinned in task 1.
3. **An attachment name with `/` or `..`** must never leave the plan's folder. Pinned in task 6.
4. **A member who is not in the household's `members`** but can reach a route (a stale session) — plan routes must go through `activeStore`, as all routes do. Pinned in task 4.
5. **Claude's research proposes `create_plan` with a parent that is itself a child** — the tool must refuse before the offer is written, not at approval. Pinned in task 3.
